Welcome to the practice

A calmer kind of dental visit

Valley Bridge Dental Practice has been caring for Scarborough families for decades. Our two partners, Dr Andy Tannahill and Dr John McCourt, both graduated from Manchester University and have looked after local patients here ever since.

Every check-up is carried out under magnification and includes an oral cancer screening, with radiographs taken only when clinically necessary. Our resident hygienist, Kathy, supports your gum health as part of a complete treatment plan.

You'll find us on the Filey side of Valley Bridge, opposite the Cask Inn — with parking available via the disc zone.

Designed around you

Comfort in the chair matters.

Background music and ceiling-mounted TVs in many of our surgeries help the time fly by. We combine a relaxed environment with thorough clinical care, so you can feel informed and at ease.
